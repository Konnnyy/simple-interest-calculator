# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator that calculates the interest earned on a principal amount over a given period of time.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Inputs
- Principal amount (P)
- Annual rate of interest (R)
- Time period in years (T)

## Example

If the principal is 1000, the annual interest rate is 5%, and the time period is 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

**Result:** 100
